🔑 CertLens

Paste any PEM X.509 certificate and see what it actually says: subject and issuer names, validity window, key algorithm and size, signature algorithm, SANs, key usages, and SHA-256/SHA-1 fingerprints - decoded by a hand-rolled ASN.1 parser in your browser, never sent anywhere.

  • Hand-rolled DER/ASN.1 parser - no libraries
  • RSA, ECDSA (P-256/P-384/P-521), Ed25519, DSA keys
  • Pure-JS SHA-256/SHA-1 fingerprinting
  • Every decoded field verified against openssl (104 automated checks)
  • Warnings: expired, self-signed, weak algorithms, small keys
